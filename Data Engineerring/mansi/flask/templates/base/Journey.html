AI Project Lifecycle

Every successful AI project follows a structured path from idea to deployment

1

Problem Scoping

This initial phase focuses on understanding the problem. We define the business objective, identify stakeholders, and establish clear success metrics to ensure the AI solution aligns with the end goal.

2

Data Acquisition

Once the problem is clearly defined, relevant data is identified and collected. This may include querying databases, accessing APIs, or gathering data through web scraping.

3

Data Exploration & Preprocessing

The most time-consuming stage where raw data is explored and cleaned. It involves handling missing values, removing inconsistencies, performing EDA, and transforming data into a usable format.

4

Modeling & Evaluation

Suitable algorithms are selected and models are trained. Hyperparameters are fine-tuned, and each model is evaluated using appropriate metrics to identify the best-performing solution.

5

Optimization & Validation

The selected model is further optimized and validated to ensure robustness, accuracy, and generalization on unseen data before moving to production.

6

Deployment & Visualization

A successful model is deployed into production using Flask APIs. Insights are communicated through dashboards and visualizations to deliver real-world business value.
